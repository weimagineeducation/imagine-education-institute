Opportunity screen

Say yes with confidence. Say no without guilt.

A good decision screen protects the mission when opportunity, pressure, and growth arrive. Rate each statement from 1 to 5. The score is a recommendation — not a substitute for judgment.

Already clear? Build a brief

An opportunity belongs at Imagine when it advances an educational purpose, serves a legitimate public benefit, fits our values, can be delivered with excellence, and does not compromise independence or integrity. Weak scores are useful. They tell you what to redesign before a partnership is promised.

  1. 01 · Mission fit

    This work clearly advances our educational purpose and a strategic pillar.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  2. 02 · Public benefit

    The educational benefit to students, educators, families, or schools is substantial — not private advantage.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  3. 03 · Need

    Evidence or stakeholder input shows the need is real.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  4. 04 · Distinct value

    Imagine is suited to do this work, and we can say why.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  5. 05 · Quality

    We can staff, safeguard, and deliver it with excellence.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  6. 06 · Access

    Intended participants can take part without unnecessary barriers.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  7. 07 · Economics

    Costs, funding, cash flow, and sustainability are realistic.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  8. 08 · Risk

    Legal, safety, data, and reputational risks can be managed.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  9. 09 · Capacity

    We have owners for the work, and it will not displace higher-priority commitments.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  10. 10 · Impact

    Outcomes and measures are defined before we launch.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true

  11. 11 · Exit

    We know when we would pause, redesign, or stop.

    1 = weakly true · 5 = strongly true
