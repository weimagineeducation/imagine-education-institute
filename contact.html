Questions we hear first

Do you only offer the workshops on the menu?

No. Popular workshops are a starting point. We customize training around local needs, and we can help you find funding.

How do you measure success?

We measure outcomes and sustainability — not participation alone. Reach, quality, learning, application, outcome, sustainability, and equity all count.

Will you work with a single school, or only districts?

We partner with schools, districts, ministries, nonprofits, and community organizations. Every partnership is designed around local needs.

Do you sell technology?

No. We are not committed to technology simply because it is new. Innovation must earn trust — ethical, useful, affordable, and sustainable.

What does a partnership actually look like?

Listen. Design. Implement. Improve. We clarify the need with local stakeholders, define outcomes and measures, model and coach, then review evidence and fit.

Can you help us find funding?

Yes. Planning professional learning includes the budget. We can help you map Title funds, grants, PTA support, and other sources against a realistic scope.
